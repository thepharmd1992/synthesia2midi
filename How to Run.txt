How to Run Synthesia2MIDI

Windows 10 and Windows 11
1) If you downloaded a zip, right-click it and choose Extract All.
2) Open the extracted folder. Keep all files together.
3) Double-click setup_windows.bat.
4) A guided installer will open. Follow the on-screen steps.
5) When setup finishes, the app will be ready to use.
6) Next time, you can double-click run.bat to start faster.

If the installer says something failed:
- Follow the steps shown on the screen.
- Use the Fix Options list (arrows + Enter) to let the installer help.
- You can also press R to retry, I to reinstall, or O to open the help page.

Mac (macOS)
1) If you downloaded a zip, double-click it to extract.
2) Open the extracted folder. Keep all files together.
3) First-time setup: right-click setup.sh and choose Open With -> Terminal.
   - If macOS blocks it, right-click setup.sh, choose Open, then click Open.
4) A guided installer will open. Follow the on-screen steps.
5) When setup finishes, the app will be ready to use.
6) Next time, you can double-click run_mac.command to start.

If the installer says something failed:
- Follow the steps shown on the screen.
- Use the Fix Options list (arrows + Enter) to let the installer help.
- You can also press R to retry, I to reinstall, or O to open the help page.
